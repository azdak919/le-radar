LE-RADAR gathers student newspapers, campus radio and campus pages from Québec cégeps and universities.

The app is for discovery and for a personal list. It does not replace the publications: the full article opens on the outlet that wrote it.

• Follow a paper, a region or a keyword
• Hide a source
• Save a card and revisit recently opened articles
• Read the last loaded feed offline
• Share the original article or the LE-RADAR card
• Listen to a campus station when an HTTPS stream is available

No account. No ads. Follows and favorites stay on the device.
