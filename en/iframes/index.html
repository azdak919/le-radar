iFrames

Two HTML frames for a campus site: the radio bar, and a sports card in IAB ad sizes. Same identity as the site — purple, Inter and Source Serif, Prochain / Aujourd’hui / Hier tags. No account.

Version française

Radio bar

The same tuner as on the site, as an opaque 62 px bar. This page’s sun/moon control switches light and dark. Pomo and Solitaire keep their own independent iframe.

<iframe
  src="https://le-radar.ca/tuner-embed.html?surface=bar&theme=dark"
  title="Campus radio — LE-RADAR.ca"
  loading="lazy"
  referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"
  allow="autoplay; encrypted-media"
  style="width:100%;height:62px;border:0;overflow:hidden;"
></iframe>

Sports cards — ad sizes

The masthead card, cropped to an IAB slot. Every 4 matches, a LE-RADAR.ca brand card. Click opens le-radar.ca in a new tab. Use ?theme=light or dark for the host site.
