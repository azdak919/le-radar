Embed

iFrames

Two HTML frames to put LE-RADAR.ca on a campus site: the radio bar, and a column of sports cards. No account. No required script.

Version française

Radio bar

The same tuner as on the site. Paste the block into any HTML page. Fixed height 62 px.

<iframe
  src="https://le-radar.ca/tuner-embed.html"
  title="Campus radio — LE-RADAR.ca"
  loading="lazy"
  referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"
  allow="autoplay; encrypted-media"
  style="width:100%;height:62px;border:0;overflow:hidden;"
></iframe>

Sports cards

The masthead CTA card, stacked. Every few results, a LE-RADAR.ca card appears and opens le-radar.ca in a new tab. Suggested width 390 px.

<iframe
  src="https://le-radar.ca/sports-embed.html"
  title="Student sports — LE-RADAR.ca"
  loading="lazy"
  referrerpolicy="strict-origin-when-cross-origin"
  style="width:100%;max-width:390px;height:520px;border:0;overflow:hidden;"
></iframe>
